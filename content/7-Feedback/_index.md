### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
My working experience at the First Cloud AI Journey community gave me a very dynamic, open, and technical-oriented feeling. Throughout the internship, whenever I encountered difficulties, I always received enthusiastic support and willingness to discuss professional matters from everyone. The professional working environment helped me maintain high concentration and gave me more motivation to successfully complete my assigned tasks.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The companionship of the mentors played a crucial role throughout my internship. The team not only thoroughly reviewed my system architecture designs but also directly pointed out areas needing optimization. Instead of providing the final solution right away, they always guided me to independently research in-depth documents and find ways to troubleshoot issues. This method helped me develop analytical thinking and problem-solving skills very effectively.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The tasks I undertook, especially designing and implementing the extract, transform, and load (ETL) pipeline, completely matched and strongly supported my Software Engineering major. The internship helped me step out of textbook frameworks to approach large-scale real-world problems, thereby expanding my mindset from simple application programming to system architecture design and cloud infrastructure operation.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
The past period has provided me with a massive amount of practical knowledge about the cloud computing ecosystem. I had the opportunity to hands-on master core services, from managing storage layers and automating data processing to building application user interfaces. Additionally, soft skills such as teamwork, technical report writing, and visual system diagramming improved significantly week by week.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
What deeply impressed me was that the culture of knowledge sharing is always promoted in all team activities. Everyone works with mutual respect and is ready to cross-support whenever system issues arise. Being encouraged to summarize practical experience into technical articles shared with the community made me feel that my personal capabilities were truly recognized and valued.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
Being provided with a well-structured system of guidance documents was a wonderful condition. The flexible working hours also helped me easily organize my work schedule proactively and achieve the highest efficiency.

  * Absolutely yes. This is truly an ideal launchpad for students who want to step out of the comfort zone of conventional coding to gain deeper access to system architecture design and professional data engineering.
